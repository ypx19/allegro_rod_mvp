Rod Rotation MVP

Three-finger axial twisting under tip constraints — what transferred, what cliffed.

MuJoCo + PPO study of DexScrew-style curricula: revolute skill → tip-connect → free tip. Constraints teach gait; removing them without a bridge fails in predictable modes.

What we found

High-confidence results from geometry audits, assist-fade runs, and mass–friction curriculum smokes (Aug 2026). Full notes live in docs/FINDINGS.md and docs/EXPERIMENT_LOG.md.

Revolute / heavy tip works

Hinge or top-hang tip-connect at high mass (s≈400) learns sustained axial twist and ω-hold. Stage 0 hanging tip also exceeds 180°.

Assist cliffs are real

Dropping the axis stabilizer or tip equality without a fade collapses to tilt / tip-error kills within a few steps—not a soft degrade.

Top-hang prefers two fingers

Best tip@s=400 policy is a cooperating two-finger gait, not three-finger surround—even when 3-contact is available.

Bottom tip stands, rarely spins fast

With 3-touch dense reward + hard window, bottom tip can stand for 20s and creep ~50–56°, but fails the ω-hold success gate; free tip (C5) died instantly.

Eval needs VecNormalize

Curriculum gates without loading vecnormalize.pkl were false negatives. Always pair checkpoints with VN stats.

Contact bonus was a silent bug

DexScrew path computed a three-finger contact term but never added it to reward until fixed—reward engineering had been flying blind.

Curriculum ablation — can degrading s actually help?

We finally tested the question directly on the simplified Allegro MuJoCo setup: same upper-tip revolute task, same 1M PPO-step budget, same final evaluation at s=1. One policy trains only at s=1. The other starts at s=400 and continuously anneals to s=1.

The story: a light rod is hard because the hand must discover contact, twisting gait, and hold stability all at once. A heavy rod buys time. Curriculum learning lets the policy discover the gait first, then carry it back to the real target physics.

Without curriculum — trained only at s=1

Same task and budget, but the hard setting from the beginning. It can spin, yet the gait is less stable and on the representative seed-1 rollout it fails the 10-second ω-hold criterion.

With curriculum — trained from s=400 → 1

The policy first learns the twisting gait in an easier heavy-rod world, then adapts it down to s=1. On the same seed-1 eval, it keeps the hold and finishes roughly 1,700° ahead.

So the answer is yes: for this revolute task, the curriculum is not just cosmetic. Under a matched update budget, continuous s=400 → 1 annealing produces a much stronger final s=1 policy than training on s=1 from scratch. This is still a single training seed and only for revolute, but it is the first controlled evidence in this repo that degrading s itself matters.

Failure mode atlas

Recurring kills. Naming them keeps ablations honest—spinning reward knobs rarely fixes a mechanical mode error.

axis_tilt

Orientation runaway under tip-connect

Spherical tip equality leaves free orientation. Without hinge / stabilizer / enough inertia, axial twist couples into tip-over. Dominant Arm B / light-mass tip death.

assist_cliff

Nonlinear removal of axis torque

Policies remain competent until stabilizer scale dips below ~0.10, then collapse. Intermediate checkpoints often beat final_model.

contact_support

Lost multi-finger window

Hard rolling requirement for 3-contact (or bonus sum). Useful after a grasp exists; lethal from the wrong reset / during transfer stages.

tip_error

Free tip leaves the workspace

C5: equality off + strong tip penalty still does not invent a fixed tip. Endpoint sails centimeters away; height terminations follow.

std_blowup

Long PPO entropy explosion

Post-peak Stage 0 with default ent_coef: policy std → astronomical, NaN actions. Infrastructure finding, not task content.

false_gate

Eval without VecNormalize

Curriculum thought top-hang tip failed until VN was loaded. Always treat metrics + videos as a pair.

What this is not. DexScrew’s published real-world finishes use revolute sim skill + tactile BC—not free-object PPO alone. Our tip-connect successes should be read as constrained skill learning, not solved freestanding in-hand rotation.

Open questions

Highest-leverage next experiments given the ledger above.

Bridge free tip slower. Anneal tip equality / solref or tip-penalty rather than hard C5 drop. Test soft tip from top-hang C4 (known spinner) before bottom tip.
Reward spin under 3-touch. Bottom C4 stands; ω-hold fails. Shape rotation only when contact_gate is green, with longer smoke budgets.
Accept 2-finger top gait? If the scientific goal is free tip, top-hang two-finger may be the right prior—not forcing surround contact.
Allegro embodiment. Mesh-free 9-DoF hand is still a stand-in. Re-run reachability + best curriculum on Menagerie Allegro once the stack is chosen.
