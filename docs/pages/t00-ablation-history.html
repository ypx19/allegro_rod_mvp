Early finding

中文

在约 10 万步截断时，带 4 帧历史的 B、D 在线回合长度（53.7 / 54.6）已经明显高于无历史的 A、C（40.0 / 46.8）。这说明在完全收敛之前，观测历史堆叠就已经有效。这还不是任务成功：评测成功率为 0，且长度不等于接触恢复。

English

At the 100k cutoff, B (192-D hist4, baseline reward) and D (192-D hist4, support-aware) already last longer online than A/C (48-D hist1). History stacking has an early length signal before convergence. This does not mean the task is solved.

Caveats that stay attached to this claim

  • 100k is not converged: returns and lengths are still rising.
  • Eval success is still 0 (80/80 axis_tilt).
  • A has the highest train return (+22) but the shortest length — reward hacking of rotation before tilt-death is plausible.
  • C/D returns are lower because of the wobble penalty, not because training failed.
  • Single seed 0. Episode length ≠ contact recovery.
  • This revises FIND-20260914-001’s “H1 not supported at history_len=4” for the 100k snapshot only: H1 has an early online-length signal, still unproven on collapse / eval metrics.
