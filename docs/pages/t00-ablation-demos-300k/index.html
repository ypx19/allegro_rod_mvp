Per-frame quantities

ω_axial = −ω·â, same sign as unwrap (rad/s and °/s). tilt θ = rod-axis angle from vertical (rad and °). tilt vel dθ/dt = finite difference of consecutive tilt angles / 0.04 s — not ω_perp. ω_perp = ||ω − (ω·â)â||, lateral angular rate (shown separately so the two are not confused). n_contact = number of fingertips with _touch() force > 0.05 N. F_i = per-tip sum of |mj_contactForce[0]| from _touch(); panel also shows the total. C/D also show gated rotation reward and low-support wobble.
